GR IIIx native-menu prototype dependencies

1. tinyblckc0000al/gr3x-fw-play
   https://github.com/tinyblckc0000al/gr3x-fw-play
   Revision: 2ad89c1f8a25b27a2068f03a394444ac418e8631
   Original additions: GPL-2.0-only; upstream portions retain their notices.
   Used from its external checkout: make_slots15, selected make_slots16 helpers,
   gr3patch_iiix, assembler interface, and native ARM emulation harness.
   We bypass the unused eager GR IV calibration load in memory, preserve all
   original icons, retain Standard MultiAxial, and limit custom curves to the
   three A-Natural entries. The upstream checkout is not modified.

2. idontknownihil/gr3-film-mods
   https://github.com/idontknownihil/gr3-film-mods
   MIT for upstream tools, with separately licensed fit data.
   Used indirectly through gr3x-fw-play's retained tool subset.
   No third-party film-fit data is used for A-Natural or IR.

3. DoYitNow/gr-custom-tool
   https://github.com/DoYitNow/gr-custom-tool
   GPL-2.0-only. Container codec subset retained by gr3x-fw-play under
   tools/gr3-film-mods/third_party/gr-custom-tool, with upstream notices.

4. Existing GRKit runtime/plugins/ir/ir.c and plugin ABI
   MIT, source included at compile time from this repository, without copying
   a second divergent implementation. Own native-cfw glue is MIT.

The generated artifact includes proprietary Ricoh firmware supplied locally.
Open-source licences for the tools do not grant rights over Ricoh's firmware.
No complete firmware or third-party source is added to version control here.
